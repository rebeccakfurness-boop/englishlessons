Two things to listen out for

1. Any moment where somebody states an opinion as though it were a fact. Those are the moments we practised spotting in our lesson.

2. Any moment where leaving something out changes the story, even though nothing untrue has been said. That is bias by selection, the same thing the two beach clean-up accounts were doing.
